LeWorldModel, replicated end-to-end — and the 11.65 pp gap we cannot explain

A from-scratch replication of LeWorldModel on Push-T and TwoRoom — 23 seeds × 50 episodes — with the gap, the data, and the honest framing. On Push-T our pooled success rate is 84.35% (95% Wilson CI 82.13–86.33, n=1150 episodes) against the paper's reported 96.0% — a 11.65 percentage-point gap with a one-sided binomial p ≈ 3×10⁻⁵⁴. A random-action baseline scores 3.33% on the same eval (n=150), so the model is doing real work; it just doesn't reach the paper's number.

The 90-second story

  • What LeWorldModel is. A Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) latent world model — ViT-tiny encoder → 6-layer predictor, 192-d latents, no pixel decoder — plus a Cross-Entropy Method Model-Predictive Control (CEM-MPC) planner that samples action sequences and scores them in latent space. Read the World Model page →
  • On Push-T, paper-budget eval. Pooled success 84.35% (Wilson 82.13–86.33) across 23 seeds × 50 episodes. Per-seed range 74.0%–92.0% (std 5.18). Read the Results page →
  • The paper reports 96.0%. Our pooled estimate is 11.65 pp below that, with a one-sided binomial p ≈ 3×10⁻⁵⁴ against the paper's claim. We list three candidate explanations and rank none above the others. Read the gap analysis →
  • A random-action policy scores 3.33% on the same eval (5/150 episodes). The replicated model is ~81 pp above random, so it is doing real work — the gap is not "the model is broken." Read the Push-T page →
  • Same model class on TwoRoom. Pooled 89.33% across 3 seeds × 50 episodes (Wilson 83.38–93.33, std 5.03). TwoRoom n=3 seeds — preliminary; not evidence of generality. Read the TwoRoom page →

The three ideas in LeWorldModel

1. A latent world model with no pixel decoder

RGB frames are encoded to 192-d latents; a transformer predictor rolls the latent forward one step at a time. There is no decoder back to pixels — the bet is that prediction in latent space is enough.

Read the World Model page →

2. Sampling-based planning in latent space

At each step the planner draws 300 candidate action sequences (horizon 5), rolls each forward in the latent predictor, scores them by a goal cost computed on latents, and refines the population with CEM over 30 iterations.

Read the Planner page →

3. An eval that confronts the gap

23 seeds × 50 episodes on Push-T, plus a 3-seed × 50-episode pass on TwoRoom and a 3-seed × 50-episode random baseline — reported as pooled success with Wilson CIs alongside the paper number and the random baseline on every plot.

Honest framing

On Push-T, our faithful replication of LeWM reaches 84.35% ± 5.18 coverage across 23 seeds, an 81-point lift over the 3.33% random baseline — but 11.65 points below the paper's reported 96.0 ± 2.83, a gap we cannot currently explain.

Three things this site does not claim: (a) that our replication reproduces the paper — the gap is statistically significant against the paper's point estimate; (b) that LeWorldModel is general — we evaluated two toy tasks; (c) that the latent predictor is internally faithful at the planner's working horizon — at k=5 the predictor's MSE is only ~8% below a predict-last identity baseline (details on the World Model page).

Every number on this page comes from outputs/demo/data/site_data.json, generated 2026-05-26. Push-T: 970 successes / 1150 episodes across 23 seeds. TwoRoom: 134/150 across 3 seeds. Random: 5/150 across 3 seeds.
