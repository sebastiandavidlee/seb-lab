What this is. This treemap shows 8 unsupervised clusters discovered in LeWorldModel's 192-d latent space (k-means over 5,000 Push-T frames), and the 3–6 sub-clusters inside each. Clusters are unsupervised. We never told the model what a phase is. Anything coherent is structure the model invented from predicting the next latent. Hover a cell to see its medoid frame and the mean block pose of its members. Click to zoom into sub-clusters. Don't read semantic phase labels into the regions — those are derived post-hoc from block coordinates and may or may not align with what the latent is actually grouping on.

Sample frames

Method: latents extracted with the trained encoder on 5,000 randomly sampled Push-T frames, k-means (k=8) at root, k-means again (k=3 per cluster, capped to the available members) inside each root. Each cell shows the cluster's medoid (the frame closest to the cluster centroid) as its background image. Sub-clusters do not necessarily reflect anything semantic — they are just the next level of geometric structure the latent happens to expose. Region labels ("middle-center", "lower-left", etc.) are post-hoc names from block-x/block-y means; the latent did not know about them.
