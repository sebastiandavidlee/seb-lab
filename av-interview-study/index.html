AV Interview Study

A working set of notes for an interview loop at an autonomous-vehicle foundation-models team.

The loop

Three rounds, and this site is organized to match them:

  1. System design 1 — multimodal perception encoder. How would you design an encoder that fuses camera, LiDAR, and radar into a shared representation for downstream perception/prediction? Covered in Multimodal Encoder and the general framework in Sys-Design Playbook.
  2. System design 2 — ML fundamentals. Classification basics through to how model complexity and data scale interact all the way to inference-time serving. Covered in Classification and Data→Inference.
  3. Behavioral. Not covered on this site — use your own notes and the Sys-Design Playbook page's "how to think about it" callouts as a source of concrete technical stories.

A possible coding round — "find the minima of a quadratic using gradient descent in pure Python" — is covered end-to-end in Math+Code and drilled interactively in the GD Lab.

Suggested 4-week study order

  • Week 1: Classification + Math+Code. Rebuild the classification toolkit and get comfortable deriving gradient descent by hand.
  • Week 2: Data→Inference + GD Lab. Move from "what is the model" to "how does it scale and get served," then drill the coding round until it's automatic.
  • Week 3: Multimodal Encoder. This is the most open-ended round — spend real time whiteboarding designs, not just reading.
  • Week 4: Sys-Design Playbook + Glossary sweeps + mock interviews. Consolidate the framework, drill terms you still hesitate on, and run at least two full mock loops.
